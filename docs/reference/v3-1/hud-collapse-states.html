HUD CARDS
Small when you want the view, full when you want the details
Every card folds into one slim pill: the portrait, both skill buttons and a chevron. The skills stay one click away, and every skill can explain itself.
Four states of the collapsed card
Your turn, skills ready
The ring and the dot on the portrait show whose turn it is. A green dot on a skill means ready.
Skill selected
The chosen skill gets a team-coloured ring and a glow. Clicking it again cancels, as today.
Cooling down
The icon dims, a ring fills as turns pass, and the number shows the turns left. The same as the expanded card.
Waiting for the other player
No ring, dimmed skills. They cannot be used, but hover still shows what they do.
Reading a skill: hover or keyboard focus on a button

How collapsing works
Each card has its own round chevron button, 44 px, named after the card.
Press C to collapse or expand both cards at once. The choice is remembered on this device.
Collapsed skills stay real buttons: select, then pick the plot, exactly as today.
The fold takes about 180 ms, and is instant for players who ask for reduced motion.
Phone width keeps the slim bottom bars you already have.
How skills explain themselves
Expanded card: the description is always written under the skill.
Collapsed card: hover or keyboard focus opens the tooltip.
Touch: press and hold for a moment to read it, a normal tap still selects the skill.
The tooltip never blocks a click, never covers the pill, and closes with Escape.
One description table feeds the tooltip and the expanded card, with numbers read from the game config.
